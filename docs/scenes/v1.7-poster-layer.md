# v1.7: the poster layer (what a guest sees before the scene is live)

The poster layer is the static image shown before the 3D scene is live: while it loads, through the 180 ms crossfade
to it, on the Static tier, after a WebGL context loss, when the scene fails to load and for a saved answer before load.
The v1.7 Sports judge (`docs/qualification.md`, the decline's judging, tells 1, 2 and 5) found it no longer agreed with
the live stage once a scene filled its frame (Sports' hero fill 0.6 → 0.92; Dinner's close-up card at 55 % of the
frame's width). Branch `v1.7/posters` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/v17-posters`, port 5706), from `v1.7/integration` at
`43105ab`. Evidence: `evidence/v1.7/posters/`. The posters themselves are the scenes' and were not touched.

## The three defects and their causes

1. **Fullscreen: the edge feather faded content.** The poster (the square still of the square fit) sits on the frame
   square over a backdrop still and was feathered over 9 % of the frame on all four edges into it
   (`src/ui/shell.css`). The backdrop was the bare studio (content hidden), so wherever content came within 9 % of
   the frame's edge it faded towards an empty cove: Sports' board top (2.5 % under the frame's top at fill 0.92) and
   the lower half of the made and host balls (2.2 % over the bottom), Business's lanyard (it runs out of the top of the
   frame), every set's cast shadow and Dinner's plate where they cross the edge. On the phone the frame square spans
   the screen's width, and its left and right edges, the stage's own, were feathered too.
2. **Embed: the square poster was cover-cropped.** A square-layout mount the host sizes to another aspect refit the
   live camera to the mount's aspect (`stage.setFrameInsets(null)`), while the poster, a square still, was laid over
   the whole mount with `object-fit: cover`. At 3:2 the cover crop enlarges the square 1.5× and cuts its top and
   bottom sixths (Sports: no ball in host-ready, guest-ready and accepted, the board's top cut); at 3:4 it cuts the
   sides (the declined ball halved). Worse, the two compositions differ: the live fit at the mount's aspect is neither
   the cover nor the contained square (at 3:4: Birthday and Business match the cover, Sports is 2 % off it and 15 px
   aside, Party 13 % smaller, Dinner's rest 24 % smaller, its close-up the contained square), and no data-free
   placement of a square still can match it before the scene has loaded (the fit needs the scene's points).
3. **The hoop's shadow popped in at the crossfade.** The bare backdrop has no shadow on the cove; the live view does,
   and at fill 0.92 Sports' hoop shadow spills well past the frame square (5.6 % of the pixels outside the square
   over 24/255 at laptop; Party's lid and Birthday's cake shadows 0.5 %, Business's lanyard 0.7 %).

## The fix

Three parts, each the simplest that holds for every scene.

**A. The frame square in both layouts** (`src/core/controller.ts` `updateFrame`, `resize`; `src/ui/shell.css`). The
square layout publishes the frame square too, with no insets: the centred square, which at 1:1 is the whole stage
(nothing changes there: the same fit through the same camera; at 720 × 720 the poster layer is pixel-identical before and after in all 20 poses, the live stage in 18, Sports' other two in ≤ 5 pixels). A
square-layout mount of another aspect is framed exactly as fullscreen frames it (`stage.setFrameInsets(NO_INSETS)`),
so the poster lands on the square the canvas draws the object in, over the backdrop. This changes the live view of a
non-square square-layout mount (a host override of the documented 1:1 stage): at 3:2 it is the same as before for
every scene but Dinner (its rest view keeps the square fit's slide instead of re-centring; its close-up holds the card
at 55 % of the square's width, not of the mount's, so 2/3 of the old size), at 3:4 every object is drawn at the
width's square, smaller than the old fit at the mount's aspect (Birthday, Business and Dinner's decline 25 %, Sports
24 %, Party 14 %, Dinner's rest 7 %, the close-up unchanged) with studio above and below
(`crops/live-composition-nonsquare-embed-before-top-after-bottom.jpg`). It is the one placement a still can match
before the scene loads, and it is the rule fullscreen already promises ("more studio, never a different
composition"). The alternative, keeping the per-aspect fit, would need per-aspect posters or shipped fit data and
would still miss by the dolly's parallax where the fits' distances differ. **For Surya to confirm** (it is a live
composition change, though only off the documented 1:1 contract).

**B. The feather inside the framing rule's clearance, on inner edges only** (`POSTER_FEATHER` in
`src/core/controller.ts`; `.lt-stage__poster.is-feathered` in `src/ui/shell.css`). The feather is half of
`PATH_CLEARANCE` of the frame (2 %): the framing rule keeps every hero and path sample at least that far inside the
frame (NDC 0.96), so the feather never crosses anything that moves, for every scene, whatever its fill (Sports keeps
2.5 % at 0.92, 5.8 % at the polish's 0.85). Only edges that lie inside the stage are feathered
(`--lt-poster-feather-left/-top/-right/-bottom`, 0 on an edge that is the stage's own; the 1:1 embed is not masked at
all). Of the three options in the brief this is the computed one, and it needs the third to hide the seam.

**C. Backdrops with the scene in them, one per pose** (`scripts/capture.ts` `captureBackdrops`;
`src/demo/posters.ts` `backdropUrl`; `assets/scenes/*/backdrop*.webp`). Each backdrop is now the scene at its pose
(v1.4–v1.6: the bare studio), rendered as before at 7 × the field around the same framing, 2000², WebP q0.92, from a
fresh page as a guest reaches the pose, with the posters' config: `backdrop.webp` (guest-ready, and the fallback),
`backdrop-host-ready.webp`, `backdrop-accepted.webp` (Dinner's on its close-up, as before) and
`backdrop-declined.webp` (Birthday's and Dinner's at their pull-back, as before): 20 files, 17–38 KiB. What the live
view shows outside the frame square (Sports' hoop shadow, Party's lid, Birthday's cake shadow, Business's lanyard,
which swings with the badge once answered, Dinner's plate) is there before the scene is live (item 3), and the 2 %
feather blends the poster into the same picture (like with like; only the static set crosses it). The controller
fetches a backdrop only where the stage shows more than the frame square, and shows it only under a poster that has
loaded (`is-waiting`): never alone (its centre is a still of one pose at a seventh of the poster's resolution), never
under the text fallback, and a new backdrop waits for its own poster.

Considered and not taken: a per-scene feather table (stale with every fill change, and still fading the set's
shadows into a bare cove); feathering only outside the content's hull (needs per-poster masks); a bigger backdrop
(2800 px at q0.85: +25 % bytes for a barely sharper lanyard; 2000 at q0.92 kept).

## Guarantees kept

- No layout shift at the crossfade: the poster sits on the square the canvas frames, in both layouts; the backdrop
  under it stays until the canvas has faded in (`is-hidden`'s delayed hide, unchanged).
- The Static tier, a context loss, a load failure and a saved answer show a complete composition: the poster and its
  pose's backdrop (the scene in it) over the whole stage; a poster that fails shows the text fallback on the stage
  colour (no backdrop under it).
- Reduced motion: the harness runs reduced; the poster is placed at once, as before.
- A 1:1 embed (the documented square layout, the gallery) is unchanged: no mask, no backdrop fetched, the same live
  view.
