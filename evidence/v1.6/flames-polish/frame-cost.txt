Frame cost, v1.6 flames polish. M2 Pro, Chrome/Metal (ANGLE), headless, the live stage; each frame
waited on with a 1-pixel readPixels; alternating 12-frame batches per case (20-30 batches),
medians. The machine was shared with other owners' servers and browsers: absolute times run high,
batch-to-batch variance ~0.2-0.4 ms; the differences are the costs.
Cases: full; nofx = no flames (volumes + twins), no bloom, no haze, no smoke (volume, ribbons);
nobloom; nohaze; nosmoke = the smoke volume hidden; skip-* = the volume's passes skipped.
