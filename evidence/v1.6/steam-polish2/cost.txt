flow map < 0.1, light volume 0.1, resolve 0.1, the march 0.8-1.0.

What paid for it (fix 7):
  - no bisection where rays enter the steam: lanes bisected at different steps and serialised their
    SIMD groups: ~0.9 ms at 0.6 s on its own (the jittered start and the history find the edge as well);
  - the scene-depth target is kept while the steam is only paused (a client's holdDepth: a steam clock is
    alive), not freed and reallocated: the bench toggles the steam every frame (0.4-0.5 ms of its "not the
    march"); a scene switch or the end of the steam still frees it at once;
  - a checkerboard march packed into a half-width target (every lane useful; a plain checker discard saved
    nothing), the other half rebuilt by the resolve from the history and the marched neighbours;
  - budgets by phase: 330 k pixels for the young cloud's fine edge, 200 k once the cloud is thin, x0.45
    with two clouds; steps dense 18 (to 0.32 s of the thermal), thin 14, veil 12; adaptive steps in dense fog;
    early exit at 2 % transmittance; probes 5; the light volume 7 + 3 (+ 4 down) taps; bisections 11/10/8;
  - the peel's fetches only within its reach of the boundary, its finest only near it, faded out by 0.45 s of
    the thermal; the billows' own-shade tap to 0.3 s of the thermal.
